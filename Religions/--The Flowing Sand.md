## History


This religion is a big and well known organization from all around the world as they have influence in every corner. This religion is mainly known by the trials that one must go though for them to be a "Sand bunch" and eventually get to the "Hourglass" rank which is a prestigious position within the religion. One may be a follower of this religion, and within followers there are prestigious followers, but only few can make it to a "Hourglass" rank. These people believe that the ones who have passed the trials are people chosen by the same [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md) to protect the flow of Time. After passing the trials one will go to training where they'll become strong warriors and will devote their lives to the Goddess. The organization has a uniform that consists of a white robe with black stripe in the middle, they also use a rope as a belt which represents how everything has a beginning and an end affected by time, and they have a. yellow humeral that one will also find the holy symbol of the Goddess of Time (a bronze hourglass) in the form of a military medal.
