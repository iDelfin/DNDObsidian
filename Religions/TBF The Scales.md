## Souls, life after death
- All life has a different "weight" that represents their whole life that can be access anytime by praying
- In death the [Deity of Death](Deity%20of%20Death) puts your "weight" into the Judicium Scale to see how the mortal has lived their life
- Depending on how the scales move compared to a simple tree leaf
	- **Lighter**: Reincarnation
	- **Same**: Eternal peace
	- **Heavier**: Torment/Punishment

## How to live a good life
- Let the Judicium spread its justice
- Support the Judicium at all cost
- Good actions with positive effects to other will lighten your "weight"
- The bigger the effect of the action you do the bigger the effect on your "weight"

## Perceptions of Divines
- Fear the Gods as they punish with no mercy
- The Gods are omnipresent, nothing can be hidden
- The Gods move the scales of the world, they communicate this to us to test are loyalty and devotion

## History

In the beginning of times the world was in a unstable condition of conflict. Two groups in eternal conflict, one more unjust than the other. Doing unspeakable action of torture and massacre. After some time something unprecedented happened, the world started to crumble and formed a platform in the form of a scale. Although it seams like there where less people on the unjust side, the platform moved as if they were heavier. A situation that has been named [[Judicium In Hoc Tempore]].
During all of this, a single mortal turn it's goal to spread the message and effect that the justice in the world has, this mortal was [[Ava Vidal]]. When Ava arrived to the land of [Farlon](Farlon.md) she traveled all the continent with a small group of followers and found a sign. A symbol that could only mean that they are being protected by the same greater power responsible of the [[Judicium In Hoc Tempore]]. A natural water spring which in top of the opening laid a T shaped rock which bottom part smoothed from all the water, creating a natural occurring scale. And with this, the [[Id Statera Praesenti]] was discovered. Around this divine object Ava opened the first [[Courtroom]], a place dedicated for gratitude to what happened in the land of union and what was given to them.

- A big way to know the purity of a soul

	- Depending on the side of the scale is the goodness of a person
- One may worship this order by
	- Connecting to a scale, one may ask for guidance
	- There is a chance that it presents itself as a vision or a "weight".
