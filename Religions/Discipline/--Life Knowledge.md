## Deities
- Deity of Aging
- Deity of the Watcher

This Discipline is one that acknowledges and accepts that life is a finite resource that all creatures must be greatly grateful of and also must make of it the most. They believe that for one to achieve great satisfaction, one must obtain the most knowledge he can get, watching great historical events or learning about them, know the reasons of a political decision or learning the past of a place, etc. A discipline that train their monks to be agile to move around abandon places or sneak about.
