## Wild Magic
### Determination of when will magic will become Wild
- Distance to a portal to the `Feywild`
	- All magic originates from the `Feywild` (Except for the cases of Druid, Clerics & Warlocks) and it is shoot outwards like particles of air without order.
	- When Sepherin casts a spell it's very difficult for her magical powers to call the specific magic she wants and that's why it may go wild
	- When Sepherin get's close to anything with some `Feywild` trace, the possibility of her magic going wild lowers because the object will work like antennas to her magic.
#### Feywild influence
```
| Creature from | Object with traces | Object recentlly exited | In or near a portal |
| ------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------- | ------------------- |
|       0       |         5          |     <-10(For 1 min)     |         20          |
```

- Emotions have a strong effect on the possibility of magic going wild
	- Emotions have a really powerful effect in the magic of the world like you could not imagine

#### Value of Emotions
```
| Happines | Saddness | Anger | Terified | Surprised | Disgusted |
| -------- | -------- | ----- | -------- | --------- | --------- |
|    0     |    10    |  20   |    50    |     5     |     0     |
```

### Possibility of going wild
25 + Emotion - `Feywild` influence 

### Over powered Wild Magic
If when throwing the 1d100 dice to determine if the spell goes wild or not, lands in a 100, then the power of spell that is thrown by Sepherin will double. If this spell is a one of a blast, Sepherin will be ejected backwards 1 square.
