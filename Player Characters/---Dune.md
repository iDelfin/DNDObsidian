### Creation of the creature [---Dune](---Dune.md)
It was a normal day in the world of Direm, magic was flowing as usual, the creatures were living there life a normal [--The Flowing Sand](--The%20Flowing%20Sand.md) were doing the goddess duty, but the still felt to disconnected from the mortal world. She felt limited on how she would obtain info from the mortal world as the only way was by directly asking a mortal (which she didn't like, she doesn't like to talk to mortals) or by spying though empowered hourglasses and she didn't want to ask [---The God_dess of Balance](---The%20God_dess%20of%20Balance.md) as she doesn't want to be in their debt. So she started thinking on ways to have a better connection with the mortal world without making it a big event or making it obvious. Then, an idea come to here, she would create a champion of hers, a creature that would tell her anything and she would like to talk to it. A creature that wouldn't be mortal, a creature that would tell her exactly what she wants to know. So she got the idea of creating a living empowered sand how would communicate with her and be devoted to her no matter what. So the Goddess went to the [---The Goddess of Life](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Life.md) to ask for a favor, for the creation of a new creature, a creature made of sand and empowered with the essence of time. At first the [---The Goddess of Life](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Life.md) was reluctant but then [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md) mentioned the Ajolotlacs and how beautiful they were, basically praising her work to convince her, witch actually worked. So they went to the desert in [Ojyolco](Ojyolco.md) and started working on the creation of the creature. [---The Goddess of Life](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Life.md) made this creature a living being, focusing he power on a bunch of sand which thanks to the great power of the Goddess, it became a crystal. It gave it a conscience and feelings but as this creature was made from only sand, feelings can't flow like blood, so they are trapped on the center of the creature, in his crystalline core. Then [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md) applied her influence into the crystal so that the creature could have the same properties as an empowered hourglasses which is represented as the objects around the crystal which makes it look like a gyroscope. After that it was just question of making nature do it's invisible force and let sand become a creature. and so [---Dune](---Dune.md) raised from the sand and is considered by the goddess as the Son of Time and her Champion.
