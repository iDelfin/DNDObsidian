# Overview
---
This operation has the objective to capture the creature that was reported by the scout present in the [Bells in the snow](Bells%20in%20the%20snow.md) mission. The questions that wants to be answered are: 
- *How did something pull an emotion to the mind of a great warrior with no feelings?*
- *How did it make it disappear?*
- *How does it work?*

This operation only entails the capture of the creature that, after some inter from [Godfrey Caramitru](Godfrey%20Caramitru.md), is commonly known as [Micixhua](Micixhua.md) 

# Beginning
---
[[Alina Vasiliu]], the second in command, has receive the initial report of the scout in the [Bells in the snow](Bells%20in%20the%20snow.md) and did some digging in the intel that they have gathered. She came across a report from long ago that dismissed a folktale from a village in [Ojyolco](Ojyolco.md) that tells of a monster that would bring sorrow without reason.
After hearing about the report and looking at this new information, she send [Godfrey Caramitru](Godfrey%20Caramitru.md) to [Ojyolco](Ojyolco.md) and ask locals about it. When he knows enough he will embark to [Torania](Torania.md) and report the location of the damaged creature
