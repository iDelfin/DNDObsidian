**Halfling** 
- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)

- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)

- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)

## Resulting races
These races came to be after the events of the [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md) and came to the world when the 4 continents came to the world as creatures living in them.

**Dwarves**
- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)
- They where creatures created by the Gods to populate the continent of Torania. They lived in the mountains that surround the continent and throughout time created massive mines and castles. Little by little they began to live in society and now some mines have been replaced by ice expeditions
**Half-Orcs**
- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)
- The Orcs came to be in the world when Inglomass emerged. As time passed, the Orcs lived around society and Half-Orcs came to be. Now there are little to non Orcs known to exist
**Ajolotlac**
- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)
- They first appeared as salamander-like creatures in some rivers on Oyolco with the distinction of having a high concentration of magic. After some time the creatures started to evolve rapidly and appeared in society
**Lizardfolk**
- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)
- They first appeared as lizard-like creatures in the borders between the dessert and the mountains, later more creatures appeared further in the dessert next to the oassis. This creatures had the distinction of being able to talk, climb walls and some started to walks in two legs. After some time, they evolved and appeared in society
**Kenku**
- Origin: [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md)
- Continuation: Curse of the Kenku
- This creature came to the world when the continent of Farlon emerged. As time passed they created a society and developed a community of flight based villages. Then they were framed of making hideous acts by another race (for them unknown, but were vampires) and cursed to not speak voluntarily and only mimic sounds
