## The material plain
The material plain is the place where most living creatures reside. The place where the effects of physics, chemistry manifest. The place where magic is manipulated and controlled by a select few that have some special conditions met.

## Xochitlan
Xochitlan: Jardín (Nahuatl [Dictionario](https://aulex.org/nahuatl/?busca=Jard%C3%ADn))
- The place where the [Kuauteponaualotl](Kuauteponaualotl.md) is located and is filled with pure magic and a safe haven for any magical creature to live in.
- The only way to enter this plain is though the [Kaltenteuilotl]
- Any type of flora and fauna in this plane helps the one who consumes it to maintain and grow its magic
- For the creatures that do not have a magical connection or their connection is small, then the consumption of the flora and fauna may overwhelm the user and create a wild magic surge.
- Relevant locations in this plane are the followings: [The Kuauteponaualotl Trunk (4), Tomb of the Fallen, Naualotlipakan (naualotl (Magia) -ipan (en) -kan(lugar))].
