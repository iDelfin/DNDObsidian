## Behavior of feelings
---
#### Normal behavior
- When the "sac" sends out the feeling, they start traveling though the walls of the veins and have this path finding behavior where their objective is to travel the whole body in the most efficient and less costly way.
- They can avoid holes that appear on a vein
- If a vein is cut entirely, the feelings will consider that place of cut as the end of their path
#### Separation of body behavior
- If an extremity is cut of a creature and there are feeling on that extremity, they stay for a while in there swirling all around, trying to get to all the parts of the body but nothing is telling them that there is no other parts of the body
- After some time, there is nothing that recharges the feelings and it stop moving throughout the body and looses its properties and becomes stale feelings

Cut out part of the body, all the feelings that were in the part in that moment will stay there for some time, later it'll evaporate and even return to the original host.

One can trick this behavior by continuing the rout of a vein with something similar to the wall. They avoid cuts but not dead ends
