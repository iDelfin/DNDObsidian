`World laws and systems`

This ritual is a way to connect two magic users and transfer some abilities from one creatures to another or from one source of magic to an item.
## Creation of the Jolín pair items
For the creation of the pair of items to have the abilities to transfer magic between creatures, one must do the following steps:
1. Get two items that will be in possession of a creature
2. Get Kuauteponaualot essence
3. Submerge both items in the same bowl with the Kuauteponaualot essence
4. The two creature must hold hands around the bowl where the two items are
5. Both creatures must concentrate on the moment they where able to do magic
	1. If the case is that they were born with the ability, then they must concentrate on what gave them that blessing Ex. Dragonborn must concentrate on their Dragon father/mother.
6. When both creature have concentrated, both items will glow and the essence will "Catch fire".
7. When the fire resides and the only thing left on the bowl are the items and a transparent viscus liquid (Kuautepontli Water). The ritual is completed
### D&D Game details
What happens is that the creatures may add one spell from each other with it's own spell slot. Both spells must be from the same level. For example: A Wizard having Misty Step and a Warlock having Shatter, may access each other spell. Now the wizard can cast Shatter and the Warlock can cast Misty Step

## Creation of Jolín item
For the creation of an item having the ability to have magic from a different place of the tree, one must do the following steps:
1. Get an item that will be in possession of a creature
2. Get something that will represent the area of magic that you want to receive
3. Get Kuauteponaualot essence. If the power desired is great, the essence amount must be relative to the power
4. Submerge both items in the same bowl with the Kuauteponaualot essence
5. The creature must have contact with the bowl
6. The creature must concentrate on the moment they where able to do magic
	1. If the case is that they were born with the ability, then they must concentrate on what gave them that blessing Ex. Dragonborn must concentrate on their Dragon father/mother.
7. When concentration is achieved, the item will glow and the essence will "Catch fire"
8. When the fire resides and the only thing left on the bowl is the item and a transparent viscus liquid (Kuautepontli Water). The ritual is completed

### D&D Game details
What happens is that the player will have an item that is enchanted with a spell and may cast it an amount of times equal to their spellcasting modifier on the moment it was enchanted - the Level of the spell (minimum of 1). The higher the level, the harder it is to enchant the item.
