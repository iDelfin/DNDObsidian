`World laws and systems`

As one starts to do unspeakable acts of magic or exhaustive use of it in one go, their Flower in the Kuauteponaualot starts to corrupt and the user will start to get physical consequences thanks to the withering of the flower. The effect are additive

| Level of withering | Effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :----------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1                  | Darkness starts to consume the area of the heart of the creature and it visibly expands though the veins.<br><br>The player has disadvantage in attack rolls with magic & targets will have advantage on saving                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 2                  | Darkness consumes more of the creature and the expansion of the veins is longer. You start to feel little numbness in your extremities. Another effect that this has is that you start to forget feelings, or stop having them. The longer you don't do something about it, more feeling will be forgotten<br><br>Your spell slots are halved down, this mean that if a player have 1 spell slot then they'll have 0 s. Just instincts of not dying and basic needs.<br><br>Your ability to spell cast is striped, you no longer have control of your character, Your death becomes eternal darkness. ## Cures
The cures will depend on the level of withering but if one gets to level 3 it cannot be reversed, that creature will forever be a being deprive of feeling, purpose, any life, just pure instinct

### Level 1 cure
This level of Withering is manageable but still a curse to have. Luckily thanks to it being just the first level, its easy to get a cure, compared to the second level.
**Re-connection with you're magic origin (Small minigame, `think`)**

### Level 2 cure
This cure is a bit more difficult as the flower is in the brink of withering away. One will feelin like it's life essence is being sucked out of existence but they're just feeling their body not being able to receive magic and a lack of feelings.
**Tend your flower with Kuauteponaualot essence**
One must first get some Kuauteponaualot essence, then they must go to any place that has been affected by the touch of it and perform a ritual similar to the root ritual, but you'll be the one with the need of the direct connection. 
1. Get Kuauteponaualot essence
2. Go to the place affected by Kuauteponaualot
3. Bathe yourself in the essence
4. Touch any directly affected part
5. Think of you origin of magic
6. The essence will start to glow and the glow will pulse towards the creature
7. Remember your feeling when gaining the ability to spellcast
	1. This is the tricky part because you're feelings are slipping away, so you must remember the feeling. One must make a DC 10 History/insight check for a still present feeling or a DC 15 History/insight check for a missing feeling. On a fail, the connection won't be successful.
	2. In case of born spellcasters, the feeling towards their father/mother
8. No matter the outcome (successful or failure) the essence will become a transparent viscus liquid (Kuautepontli Water).
