## Satupatet
(Latin) satus - inicio, patet - planicie
This is the name of the original continent that made up Direm and the place where the first sapients came to the world.

# Races included in this origin
- Humans
- Vampire (Act II only their separation of humans and physical properties like their pail skin, but no vampire features like blood need and sunlight harm)
- Halfling
- Elves (Act II)
- Dwarves (Act III)
- Half-orcs (Act III)
- pre-ajolotlac (Act III)  

# Origin Act I The suffering of cold
- Center continent with all types of climate
- sapient life began on the cold part of the continent
- Resources where scarce and food was limited but enough to maintain them alive. (RN.1.1)
- The sapients saw a warmer and lushed valley on the other side of a lake, but could not cross it as currents were strong
- As time passed, people live, people died and suddenly, the cold became si much that the lake froze and the ice became thick enough to cross it by walking (RT.1.1)
- The sapients crossed and arrived to a warmer place where they need not to worry about death by the climate
- Some Humans that were inside a cave to avoid freezing to death, where left behind as they dared not to cross (future vampires)
- The sapients grew closer and cherished every bite of food and thanked Nature for providing and Time for creating. (R.1.1)

# Origin Act II The duality of peace and war
- The sapients live in harmony for a long period of time on this new land that was discovered on the other side of the lake.
- During this time some Humans concentrated all their effort to this peace that they were experiencing and made a stronger connection to their emotions. These creatures eventually evolved and became the Elves
- Civilization started to grow and thrive and Time and Nature became respected and beloved as gods
- Then, disaster, the Humans who where left behind because off their fear of leaving the cave (called Vamps), appeared and started attacking with hatred and anger as they felt betrayed. Bringing primitive weapons to the fight
- During this fight the first signs of magic came to manifest. The Vamps imbued there weapons with magical properties which manifested in vain like traces on their weapons (This thanks to the amount of hatred an anger in them)
- Not the only ones with signs of magic as the Elves also manifested the first magic in the form of magical shield appearing in small specific places. A very effective spell that doesn't need a lot of energy
- This is when something happened, earthquake started and skies darkened. A physical rupture appeared and divided the Vamps and society.
- A vision was given to the members of the society. `A great rupture in the land will appeared and the earth will spit out anger, followed by a flood to balance the world again, but this will mean the end of the land. We also give you the knowledge to dominate the plain of waters and survive`
- This vision was seen as a punishment towards the Vamps for their actions in the introduction of war to society and with that, the introduction of a new god. The god of punishment and justice. (RP1.1)
- The society started to build various boats for all to leave Satupatet and survive the disaster

## Origin Act III The end and the beginning
- Society started to board the boats to prepare the destruction of the land. They launched and left their home
- The people in the boats were really varied, but there were a strong tendency of people with stronger believes on the different topics they have seen throughout their time. Time, Nature and Justice.
- They all tried to stick together after launching but thanks to the lack of practice in boat driving XD and the extreme condition that were going on around
- An earthquake started happening and the rift opened wider making many flora and fauna being swallowed. A new rifts started to form around the continent in a slow but constant manner.
- The rift now started to crashed and pushed stone and earth created a mountain which then started spiting lava destroying and burning everything in its way.
- Then the flood came. Giant waves started to get near the continent, as the Society tried to survive the harsh conditions of the sea, the giant waves were the biggest challenge and unfortunately separated the boats.
- The waves crashed into the continent, pushing it underwater and with the rift that was forming around, this made the continent disappear under the water,  under the earth, lost for eternity
- The Vamps ran away to their caves and tried to survive the punishments given by the gods. The fate of the Vamps was unknown to all but them as it was safe to assume that they all died with the disasters
- The Society watched in agony as they saw their home be consumed by the faiths of the gods, but had to venture forward and survive
- The gods blessed them with more blessings. The food they had to survived took longer to go bad to the point that it never did and gave more energy than before.
- As time passed, small earthquakes were felt and with that new continents rose to the surface and formed what we know as Direm with the four continents
	- The people with a higher concentration of Time beliefs saw as a continents rose from the waters being carried by what seems to be a spectacular dragon with glorious wings only to be obstructed by the great mountain. This was the continent of Inglomass
	- The people with a higher concentration of Justice and Punishment beliefs saw another continent rise from the waters in a constant velocity and around it, a powerful current circling the land mass, but the people witness that the responsible of the rise of the land and the current was a giant snake making a circle to work as a screw for the land. This was the continent of Torantia
	- The people with a higher concentration of Nature beliefs saw as flora started to grow in such a rapid pace that it started pulling land from the sea to the surface and flying over this fauna, a fathered serpent dropping what seems to be seeds and powders to the land and going towards the center of the lake. This was the continent of Ojolco
	- The people with less beliefs concentration but still some belief, saw how this strong current moved in an infinite shape. Then pieces of land started to rise from the water and crashing together, forming high mountains and rocky terrains. After that, a huge dragon was seen emerging from the sea and posing in the sky just for it to dive back down and disappear, This was the continent of Farlon 
- The respective people turned their boats to those lands to arrive to the new lands
- With this new land, the goddess of Nature and the goddess of Time gave the Society new members to always remember the unity they used to have
	- For the people of Inglomass, when they arrived, they were surprise to see a land filled with beautiful grasslands, forest and mountains filled with life and perfect for creating a society. With this new land came new life as near the mountains a new type of race emerge, the Orcs. While a very isolated race, they still interacted a lot with the society and eventually the half-orcs came to creation.
	- For the people of Torania, when arriving they were surprise to find a land filled with cold like temperature but with forest filled with trees as strong as metal and beautiful creatures roaming around a lake in the center of it all. With this new land came new life as inside the caves of Torania emerge the first Dwarves(Race one) (Merfolf?)
	- For the people of Ojolco, when arriving, they were surprise to find a land overflowing with nature and magic. Even the people who had a lesser connection to magic could sense it in the flora and the fauna. With this new land came new life as in the different lakes found around Ojolco one could see the salamander like creature with the ability to talk and walk outside the lakes, the first Ajolotlacs came to life
	- For the people of Farlon, when arriving, they were surprise to find a land that seams like a place full of open space and various high mountains around, warm dry temperature and on the other side of the mountains, a beautiful extensive dessert. With this new land came new life as the people noticed lizard like creature with the ability to talk, and tall crow like creatures with the same ability, but only of repeating sounds. The lizards evolved eventually to Lizardfloks and lived in harmony along society, while the crows evolved into the Kenkus and gain the ability to talk by themselves.
