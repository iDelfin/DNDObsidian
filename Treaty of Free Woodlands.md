# Agreement

> *"None of the Kingdoms involved shall claim ownership over the [[Gesomnrīce forest]]
> This is done to protect the historical value of the forest and to preserve its history for all to remember
> "In addition the Kingdoms involved shall agree to protect and preserve the forest from any damage cased by any voluntary means.
> Some voluntary means include but are not limited to:
> - Harvesting raw materials
> - Burning Flora
> - Hunting
> - Expanding or development of cities
> - etc"*
> *"Breaking this treaty can be consider as a provocation of war"*
