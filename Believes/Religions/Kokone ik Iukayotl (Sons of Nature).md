## Creation Myth
- Great seed of creation (the icon)
- Water and dust surrounded the seed
- Seed grows and created the world

## Souls, life after death
- The soul is part of nature and just one more place in the world
- When in death the "soul" will move to a different place to live like a tree or a thinking being
- While moving, the soul of the being is transported to [The appearing nature (Death)](The%20appearing%20nature%20(Death).md) as a waiting room while deciding where the soul may go.

## How to live a good life
- All should thank nature for what they receive from it (food, material, etc)
- Protect nature and it'll protect you back
- All living creatures deserve to live but all deserve to eat.
- Death is part of nature and shouldn't be feared

## Perceptions of Divines
- There are seen as parents or head families members
- As a protective body that will act as a protector, mentor and punisher if need arises
- No fear in the Divines

## Worship and service
- There is no general service in this religion, but the worshiping occurs in a more individual level and in the actions of those
- **General worshiping:** If wanted one could sit on front of a tree and could enter a trance to feel the connections of nearby life
	- During this trance, sharing anything to this system (feeling, knowledge, etc.) is a way to thank Mother ([The Goddess of Nature](The%20Goddess%20of%20Nature.md)).
- **When foraging** 
	- One would pick up all the resources and when finished they would place them on the ground, enter a quick connection with nature to ask if there is a need for what they have gotten. 
	- A way to be part of the community of nature
- **When hunting**
	- If death of the pray was immediate, one would say a small thanks while pushing their forehead to the creatures forehead
	- If no death came to the pray, then a small pray and a thanks would follow while bringing it to its death
		- "Nature thanks you for your capsule and the community will use it to grow. May mother's embraces bring you peace"
### Death ceremony
**General death**
- One would burry the body and accommodate it in a fetus position.
- Mixed seeds would be spread on top of the burial site and leave a small seed mark on the ground
- "Nature thanks you for your capsule, let your body be one with mother and find a swift wait"

**Fellow Kokone**
- One would burry the body and accommodate it in a fetus position that would hug a tree seed or flower seeds (depending on the ecosystem around the burial).
- The pendent with the symbol of the religion would be hanged on a near tree or set on top of the burial site
- "Nature thanks your protection and be at peace that it will give you a swift wait. May this tree represent the actions that you've given to Mother and protect all those in need."
