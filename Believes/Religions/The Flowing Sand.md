## Souls, life after death
- It all starts from the [Plateau of Creation] that erodes and parts of it falls into the Sand clock of time.
- While parts of the plateau falls, it's embedded with energy from the cosmos and depending on it's trajectory its if the mortals that are represented from this have magic or not.
- While falling, the debris start to dismantle from all the energy being stored and become grain of sand and enter the great clock
- All mortals are grains on the great flow of the sand clock
- This clock is what also dictates the flow of time
- We all flow, we all move and when we pass the throat of the clock, that's when our time ends.
- It is said that your actions in your life will have a direct effect on the weight of your grain and the heavier it is, the faster it'll fall thought the other grains.

- Some followers have experience [The appearing nature (Death)](The%20appearing%20nature%20(Death).md) bit seen it as a waiting room, which is correct in a way
- Some followers can see the [Great Clock] and, because of their beliefs, assume the clock is the one being the judge.

## History

This religion is a big and well known organization from all around the world as they have influence in every corner. This religion is mainly known by the trials that one must go though.
The organization has a uniform that consists of a white robe with black stripe in the middle, they also use a rope as a belt which represents how everything has a beginning and an end affected by time, and they have a. yellow humeral that one will also find the holy symbol of the Goddess of Time (a bronze hourglass) in the form of a military medal.
