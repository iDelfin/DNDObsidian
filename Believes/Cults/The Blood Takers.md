This cult is a group of people that dedicate their whole life to the Patron [--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md) in exchange of otherworldly power. They seek various lands for take over mainly caver to create a base and do investigations in the name of [--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md).

## Main objective
Their main objective if to give [--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md) freedom to roam the material plane and rule the whole world with his cult by his side.

## Current objectives
- Complete the [Blood Mood Ritual](Blood%20Mood%20Ritual.md) for testing the power of blood and the results of bringing [--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md) to the material plane
- Seeking more land to create more bases in [Torania](Torania.md) and have more control and power there.
- Creation of more "Blood gems"
- Constant recruitment of more members weather be it by self will or by force
