## Deities
- Deity of Herds
- Deity of Independence

A person that is part or follows this Discipline has the following ideal as they are related with the deities. They are people that believe that one becomes stronger when they work together with others be it companions, friends or family. You also have the ideal that no man is left behind but you also believe in the importance of someone's decision and independence, so if you go to someone that is left behind to save his companions and you go with him so that no man is left behind, but the man tells you he wants to be left behind to save his friends and to bring justice to himself, you must respect that decision. The respect of an individual’s decision is greater than any other ideal.

Their monastery is located on [Brogmog](Brogmog.md)
