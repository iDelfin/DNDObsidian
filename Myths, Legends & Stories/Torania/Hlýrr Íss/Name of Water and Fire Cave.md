## Regional Location
- [Hlýrr Íss](Hlýrr%20Íss.md)

### What is told
- Long ago a pair of explorers where looking for some exiting place to discover
- They ended up at the entrance of a cave that didn't make sense to them
- At the southern most part of the Hlýrr Íss region one can find a hidden gulf and a ramp that leads to this cave
- When entering the cave the environment felt suffocating and humid but wanting to have a discovery of their own they pushed forward
- The cave started to turn slightly but enough to dim the sun light the deeper the go inside the cave
- The suffocating environment became more aggressive the deeper they went and started to affect their judgement in decision
- One of the explorers stepped on a loose stone and fell to a small cliff but "luckily" fell on water
- But he wasn't as the moment he pulled himself to the surface of the water he started to scream in agony shouting "it buuuuurns", "Judgement why me"
- When his friend helped, he burned his hand and couldn't get in the water to help
- He says that he looked everywhere to try to pull him out from the water, but only found rocks and dirt. He could not save him
- When he returned to town he told his story and started to document about the inners of the cave and how dangerous it is and named it the [Water and Fire Cave](Water%20and%20Fire%20Cave.md)
