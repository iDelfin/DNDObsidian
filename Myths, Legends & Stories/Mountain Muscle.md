>"Legend says of an unmined Mythril vein near the surface"

> “Long ago the old Dwarfs explored the “Mountains of Heat” for resources until they found a mineral that was hard as Mythril, and rare like it. It had to be left behind as a reminder of the failed vein that the Dwarfs could not mine.”

> “The Dwarfs continued mining and collecting minerals, but something was changing. The deeper they go, the easier the mining and caring became. As if they were being embedded with a strength boost. While in the surface, they could only carry their tools, but while below they could add a variety of stones and minerals with them.”

> “The dwarfs became obsessed with the feeling of strength while being buried in the mountain. So much so that their society started to develop caves and shops deep in the Mountains of Heat. They didn’t want to leave this strength behind nor did they want to face the ‘Failed Vein’. Even tho they have the strength of thousands, there is still one vein they could not beet.”
