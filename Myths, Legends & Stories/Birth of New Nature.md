### What is told

- Long ago the fauna of the world used looked like a worldwide pandemic fell into them
- No animal wanted to live, no animal wanted to survive, they were just weak creatures without a purpose
- All this an effect of the missing Diety of the fauna
    - Some say that it died with a massive hunt that coincided throughout the world because of need
    - Other say that it was hunted by a monstrous man filled with greed and pride
    - And others say it fell ill and that's the reason the fauna also fell like a pandemic
- One quiet night a simple girl wondered onto a forest and stumbled upon the [The Goddess of Nature](The%20Goddess%20of%20Nature.md)
- They talked about how the world needed a [Deity of Life](Deity%20of%20Life.md) and offered incredible power
- The girl took the offer and with that the world of flora and fauna came back to life

### Errors in the story

- It wasn’t a pandemic, there was no highway from “The Tree” to the animals to transpose them with more magic
    - Feelings are a strong element in the connection to magic but animals do not have the emotions very developed
    - They need a magnifier, a multiplicator, to have the same level of connection to the tree as highly smart creatures.
    - Without it they were in a constant state of exhaustion, and the little energy that they had it was used to eat easy pray or vegetation
- The original Diety of the Fauna did not die because of a hunt or an illness, it was because of the Myth [Tree of Vitality, Tree of Brutality](Tree%20of%20Vitality,%20Tree%20of%20Brutality.md) and it’s defeat in battle.
- The girl did not just wondered onto the woods, she did this every afternoon, she would protect any week animals that she would see and thanks to this she gain the respect of the Goddess of Life
- The girl did not stumble upon the Goddess of Life, it was the Diety of the Hord, weaker that how he is known because of it’s dependency on the hoard of mortal creatures
- Incredible power was not offered, just the power to protect more animals
