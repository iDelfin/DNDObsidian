Once a compatriot, and now I must endure the darkness of his departure.

[Weather symbol]
Force of nature, first responder to natures calling, may your remains bring peace to the land. Hear my words, hear my soul. I rest your body in the land of your beginning. Your sacrifice has become everlasting and the ones who remain will always be in your favor. May the [ævigrœnn] (ævi- (forever), -grœnn (green)) forest protect your eternal slumber.
