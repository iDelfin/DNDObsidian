**Tablet A**

On the old time, in a world full of unity and peace, shine a single tree which lays beyond the eyes of the mortal creature but present in all living beings. But as all things, the universe must have balance. For every light there is a shadow. This tale follows the battle for defending the magic and life of all creatures in Direm. 
After the Þrígaldor (Tri-magic in [Anglo-Saxon]  [Dictionario](https://anythingtranslate.com/translators/old-english-translator/)) created the deities that look over the world, they would be soon called upon to take on their most important task, or, as others called it, their demise.
In the land of garden, where the single tree lays to witness all, the deities prepare for what is to come. A force of nature destine to reach it's opposite, attracted to the tree. One representing magic and life as we know it, and the other representing absence and nothingness. A reunion destine to end in annihilation.

**Tablet B**

On the edge of the land, the [Kaltenteuilotl] (kaltentli (puerta) - teuilotl (cristal)) [Dictionario](https://aulex.org/nahuatl/?busca=Jard%C3%ADn) opens with it's dazzling beauty, but what came from it dimmed any beauty. Pure malice and destruction. An ooze exits and starts to pile up in front of the [Kaltenteuilotl]. It start to tower all the deities and then, stops, silence invades the garden and the ooze towering over all, opposing the presence of the trees. Then, it begins, rotating different parts in different directions and forming something unnatural. A creature without form of its own, like black liquid fire, spreading to consume anything and on its path, and the deities stand on defend.

**Tablet C**

The battle is unlike anything the world has ever seen. Forces of nature clashing and annihilating each other and flashes of light blinking in an instance. Then, a unfortunate discovery was made. A deity, a force of magic and light, had direct contact with the force of malice and destruction. Annihilation. A powerful explosion originating from this interactions of opposite forces. Death of the first deity and damaging the malice. Fear starts to settle into the minds of the deities. Not only will they perish if the contact any part of the enemy, but they do not have the numbers to use this discovery to defend the tree.
As the battle continues, some perish, others get injured, but they shall not give up. The more they damage the malice with their celestial power, the weaker it becomes. They coordinated attacks, dodged incoming one and defended the tree from contact, until, a shift in the behavior of the battle. The malice deforms in various forms, it squishes, stretches, and, with a screech of what can only be link to agony, divides and throws it self onto the tree. The impact seams imminent, but from the corner of the land a noble and brave deity dives into the trajectory and receives the contact, the force of the explosion is felt throughout the garden and the mortal plane. Although most of the effect seams isolated, part of the tree became damage.

---
**Tablet D**

The result of the battle seams inevitable, an equal force of malice and light destined to annihilate each other. This led to the intervention of [Kuauteponaualotl](Kuauteponaualotl.md). Sap starts to fall from the tree, as if the tree was crying. It starts to focus on the top of the tree, then a small dark bubble appeared in the center of the battlefield and slowly expanded and encased everyone. The battle froze in place, no movement, only those outside the bubble could move. Then more sap from the tree, it focused on the top of the tree and with it, the deities that where inside the bubble started to be pulled out of it. Lastly, when all Deities where outside the bubble, sap from the tree, but this time it was as if the tree was bleeding. Focused on top and with it a new effect. The bubble became darker and darker until pitch black and with it, quickly collapsing to a single point in the center of it and nothingness. The tree send all those within the bubble to another plane, a new plane created by the tree in that very moment with the single purpose of trapping the malice for eternity.
Although the battle was finished, the losses where great. It is a day to grieve those who fell and honor those who defend our existence. For each fallen, an altar is made to remember their sacrifice and spirit. If they have not been in this world, all creature would not continue that which is their own story.
