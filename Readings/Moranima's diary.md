Day 90 in the castle
Research has come very far and I now know much from the origins of magic. What I did not expect was where they are, something hard to control and others easy to do so, but in some situations they are unavoidable like it happened to me. While exploring a bit more of the castle and it's treasures I was returning to my hut to continue the research, when I made a discovery, one I wish i didn't presence. The shadow I talked of the other day was no fiction of my imagination, but a demon. It appeared in the mess hall filled with anger and resentment and called itself [Shadow Demon Tarlock (dead)](Shadow%20Demon%20Tarlock%20(dead).md). After dodging and returning attacks that came to me, an opening appeared. I trapped the demon into a gem that I found recently, but I know it was not big enough to contain it. So I used the time that the gem gave me to anchor the demon into different places of the castle and set some pedestals to monitor the trap. I believe that the demon has a connection with my research, but I do not know how yet. When I discover that connection I'll free it and do my tests.

The gem started to break and so the first bind of the demon and with it some of its power. Then smokes of shadow blocked the doors and the light from within became blocked by some kind of smoke and all I could hear scream of agony from what I can only assume are the dead. The castle has been overrun by shadow.
