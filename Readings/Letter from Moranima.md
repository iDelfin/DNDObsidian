Dear (Player)
I write to you while on my quest to learn more about magic and yet while having no way of exiting this place, not out of discipline, but because of an ancient power that lays on this walls. I do not yet know from where it came or how it came to be but I do know that it was not here when I first arrived. If you find this letter then my life has ended and so is my research. I bestow to you the [Shard of Xochitlan](Shard%20of%20Xochitlan.md) that I found during my exploration. You can become one with it to gain its power and its benefits but there are still some secrets to be discovered with this shard. As my final lesson to you, you must uncover this secret and protect it by all means necessary. You'll find the shard inside the greenhouse, buried on a small chest next to the first magical crop you grew. Keep it safe, protect it from those with ill or selfish intentions.
I wish I could've returned to you one last time, but fate works in mysterious ways. Keep your spark lighten, as it will be your light in your darkest moments
~Moranima
