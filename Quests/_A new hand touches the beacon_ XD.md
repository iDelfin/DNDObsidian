## Summery
This side quest is divided in 3 parts. The beginning which consists on the giving of the quest and what to do. The part I which consists on the way to the cave and any adventure that happens on the way to there. And The part II which consists on entering the cave, exploring anything that can be found there, and finally, the consequences of the action of a player that can carry from a battle to a pact to a patron

The blood gem will talk to [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md) and will give him indications to go to a place and offer the gem to the circle. This voice will offer anything that a person wants, and as he has been in the pocket of [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md) for a long time he will offer answers, answers about that amulet from his former mentor.
The gem will guide [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md) by means of a thin beam of light that that only he can see thanks to a pinch that the stone made to [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md). This beam will lead the party to a cave where they will find a circle made with blood and a stone pedestal with a boul. Then a voice will be hear by anyone in the room. The voice will say (in a more demonic voice) to put the stone on the circle, and will be very insistent. If the player won't do it, the voice will start being louder and louder, the cave will start to tremble and will feel like it'll collapse on it's self. After some time, everything will become silent for some seconds (for dramatic effect) and the voice will say, "If you will not do it, I'LL TAKE IT FROM YOUR DEAD HANDS". In this moment, demons will appear in the cave from a dark mist tornado and will attack the party (Amulet will come in handy here).
## Constant
[--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md) will constantly try to tempt [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md) to use it's gem as a multiplier to his weapon. To feed the gem with the blood of it's enemies and increase the damage of it's weapon. [--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md) will also tell him to fuse it's gem to others to create a more powerful gem and with that have more power. One blood spill for now will equal to +1 damage for the next attack with a cap of 2 for now. The cap can increase the more gems they fused to this gem. 

# Beginning
---
### Location
This side quest is given after completing the dungeon of 'the castle of old' and when they exit to return to a city. The cave they will be lead to will be located at the far east of [Torania](Torania.md), past the Frozen Desert, facing the sea.

### Quest giving
The blood gem that [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md) has in his hand will talk more clearly to him, saying the following
`You must return this gem, you are the one that must do it`
`You must do this, If you do I can give you answers`
`Don't you want to know who really is your mentor!?`
`Don't you want to know what is the object you stole!?`
`If you bring me the gem, I'll give you the answer you seek`
`A favor for another`
This is non other than [--Erythroier, The Blood Eater (Elitroier)](--Erythroier,%20The%20Blood%20Eater%20(Elitroier).md) who wants the gem that [Sindri Daergel](Sindri%20Daergel.md) has because of the blood it was embedded with as it was embedded with Lycanthrope blood. A race that has been on hiding force centuries and no one knows where they are. That's why it's special and need it for a complete Blood Moon ritual.
