## Beginning
---
### Location
This side quest is given in any potion shop and will be on the mountains located south east of[Torania](Torania.md).

### Quest giving
The NPC will talk about how they received an order for some sleeping potions but they ran out of the main ingredient the best one. The plants name is **Rhododendron** and it can only be found on the snowy mountains of [Torania](Torania.md). At the same time the potion keeper will pay well for any extra plants and materials that the party brings.

## Development
---
### Summery
The party will be going to a cave in one of the caves of [Torania](Torania.md) and will have go though the treacherous environment of the cold mountains, blizzards, snow storms, avalanches and falling paths. On the way to the cave one will find various caves for them to rest and wait out any storm.

### Encounter
- If the party is Lv. 2 then they will find a White Dragon Wyrmling in the cave where they will find the plant
- If the party is Lv. 4 then they will find a Winter Wolf with a wolf in the cave where they will font the plant

## End
---
This side quest will be considered completed when the party delivers the plant **Rhododendron** to the potion keeper. The moment they get there, they will give the party the payment and any extra for other materials. By completing this quest the party will open the option of selling materials to the potion keeper and more potions will be available to them
