
## How it became a Patron
This evil Patron was ones mortal, living his own life as normal as a citizen of the [---The Kingdom of Framtid](---The%20Kingdom%20of%20Framtid.md) as a lone scholar in his town. most people would look down at him as he wasn't a great mage. Most spell he would cast would turn into disaster, from turning the arm of someone to a tentacle, to absolute obliteration of people. That obliteration was what broke him and took him into madness for more power and so he locked himself in his studies until he would find something. However during the seek of more power he started to meddle with dangerous magic, magic that would give him what he wanted. During an exploration he stumbled upon a `tome` which contains different powerful spell that could grant him immense power, all in exchange of one `Feeling` per spell. His young apprentice [[---Aprentice of The Perserver]] tried to persuade him not to use the `tome`, telling him that "it could change your whole person, y will not be the same person as you were... please don't". For a moment The Preserver felt bad and was gonna stop and burry the `tome`, but his greed for power and domination took the best of him and so he studied and studied so that he could memorize each and every single spell and destroy the tome an making it impossible for others to obtain this power.
After learning and memorizing each and every single spell in the `tome` he started to experiment with the spells and decided that the first spell he would cast was one which would give him immortality in exchange of "Empathy". So he started to carve with a burning knife in his right arm the word of "Empathy" and started to recite the incantation. Other worldly flames started to surround him and lift him into the air. [---Aprentice of The Perserver](---Aprentice%20of%20The%20Perserver) ran towards to stop him, yelling to him to stop the madness, but he couldn't listen with the sound of the flames and destruction around him. That's when [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md) appeared in front of The Perserver and prevented the spell from being completed, disrupting the incantation and unstabilizing the spell which unleashed a great amount of energy outwards and killing The preserver and unfortunately the  [---Aprentice of The Perserver](---Aprentice%20of%20The%20Perserver) in the exposition.
While in the way to see the The [Deity of Change](Deity%20of%20Change.md) he was stopped by a strange force that was preventing him to reach death. That force took him to a strange plane of existence, a sort of limbo, where he was not dead nor alive. A place where he had some influence in the material plane, but could not pass there. While there he recited one last spell he remember which is the gain of minor godly powers in exchange of Sadness. a very powerful `Felling`. When he started to carve the word, he realized that there was no blood in him nor did he feel pain, so he carved and began his incantation and what followed was the same effects as in the material plane, but more chaos and felt more powerful. He obtained godly powers which the catch that his power would be proportional to the amount of souls he had.  With this last incantation he became The Evil Patron [TBU The Preserver](TBU%20The%20Preserver.md).
Thanks to the amount of time that he has spend in the `Limbo plane` his memories from the material plane have been altered and have nearly lost all of them, but one memory that he has forgotten was the memory of all the `Feeling spells` which made him angry and so he started to look for followers to make him more powerful and dedicated on looking for other places to find these spells. He promise people in the material plane the gain of powers like nothing they have ever seen  before and a rightful place in his new world. He ask these people to assemble a cult and promise the members the same.

## Main goal
- To cross the border to the Material plane and dominate and mold the earth to chaos

## Goals
- To gain more souls to increase his influence in the mortal plane.
- Defeat and obliterates any traces of [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md). Destroy [--The Flowing Sand](--The%20Flowing%20Sand.md) and any members with in the order.
- Defeat and destroy [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md).
- Create chaos in different parts of the world

## Current influence in the material plane
- Can make pacts with people and loan some of his power by carving his `mark` into the body of the person
- Can see what is happening in the Material Plane by means of one way windows in flames
- Can send minor forces to move anything for small amounts of time
- Cast minor spells and effects 
- Can talk to people though his `speaking mark`

## Obstacles
- He does not want [---The Goddess of Time](---The%20Goddess%20of%20Time.md) to know that he is still "Alive" and so limits his actions to once that are easily masked as mortal actions.
- Currently his influence in the material plane is small and must rely in mortal to do his job

## Pacts
To have a pact with The Preserver one would be contacted by him directly as he has seen you as someone with great power and potential to help him. One would see how wall or an object is started to be scratch by nothing until it would form the `speaking mark` of The Preserver and speak to you, and only you. He would try to convince you to make a pact with him by promising new great powers and a special place in his new world.
Another way would be by promising him your full devotion to him. One would need to find a way to contact him which is achieved by carving his `speaking mark` and drooping one drop of blood. After that The Preserver will be able to hear you. One must convince him that you are "Warlock" material.
