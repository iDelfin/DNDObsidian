These mighty protectors have the important task of protecting the Navalot forest of [Ojyolco](Ojyolco.md) from any threats that would damage or destroy it. They utilize are known for their great ability to move around trees while making almost no sounds. This organization is a big group of warriors that range from mostly fighters from different races but have a high concentration of Wood Elves, Ajolotlacs and Green Merfolk but the high ranks are mainly powerful people with different specialties.
