# Music Popularity Points (MPP)
---
These are the points that one may gain during their music career in the world of Direm. There are a lot of ways one can gain these point which will help in their road to World Popularity. This points can be redeemed for various things, be it for creating things that could help to the road of popularity or gaining temporary instant boons. The amount of MPP will indicate your popularity in the world which is indicated by 4 stages (Starter, Indie, Rising Star, Star)

|       Starter       |           Rising Star            |               Indie               |                           Star                            |
| :-----------------: | :------------------------------: | :-------------------------------: | :-------------------------------------------------------: |
|       1-9 MPP       |            10-29 MPP             |             30-59 MPP             |                        60-200 MPP                         |
| You can now use MPP | Inn can now accept you for shows | You can register on Clubs to sing | You can register into arenas to sing to a bigger audience |
|                     |     Set up a Meat and Greet      |  You may set up Autograph booths  |          A constant advantage to charisma rolls           |

## Moving MPP
---
---
MPP are the way one can quantify how impactful your music is around the world and how, well, popular it is to people. To do so one must do various actions and responsibilities to increase or maintain your MPP level.
### Increase
---
#### Singing
Singing is the most direct way to spread your music to the world and show your talents to the world. Depending on how you do your singing is the impact you'll have to increase your MPP
##### Singing on the road/streets | 0 MPP
*Starter performance*
**IDK:** 20 DC
**Impact:** +1 MPP
**Downtime:** 1/4 day
There is no place that isn't your stage. Grab your instrument and play your song, the public is your crowd
##### Singing at Landmarks | 5 MPP
*Starter performance*
**IDK:** 18 DC
**Impact:** +2 MPP
**Downtime:** 1/2 day
Your songs have spread enough that you won't be bothered in important landmarks. You have even become a landmark from the city itself.
##### Singing at an inn | 10 MPP
*Rising Star performance*
**IDK:** 15 DC
**Impact:** +3 MPP
**Downtime:** 3/4 day
You music has started to become known and with it local business would love to use your talents to bring new costumers. You may be invited to play on a *nearby* $^1$ inn or have advantage on asking to play on them.
##### Singing at clubs | 20 MPP
*Indie performance*
**IDK:** 12 DC
**Impact:** +5 MPP
**Downtime:** 1 day
Your music have come to the point where musical clubs will accept you, they see your potential. You may be invited to play on some exclusive clubs that include your genre of music. 
##### Singing at arena | 60 MPP
*Star performance*
**IDK:** 10 DC
**Impact:** +10 MPP
**Downtime:** 2 days

#### Special events
This are events that you would do if you wanted to connect with your fans and do special things 

- Singing/Making shows
	- Singing outside *just the performance check*
	- Singing at an inn (Check Table) *1d8*
	- Singing at a stadium/center (Check Table) *1d12*
		- (If pass a DC 10 performance check add Charisma modifier)
- Make a positive impact to a place *1d6+Charisma modifier*
- Giving merch/autographs (only available at Indie) *1d4+Charisma modifier*

### How can one use MPP
---
#### Starter
- ==4 MPP (Bonus action)== **Stage excitement**: You remember the passion you have when you get up in stage. You and all your alias gain 20ft in their movement for one turn
- ==10 MPP (Bonus action)== **Stage face**: One must prepare for the stage and look their best. Add your Charisma Modifier to your next ability check. If the check uses your Charisma Modifier you may add it again

#### Indie
- ==6 MPP (Action)== **Star Structed**: Some consequences of popularity is that people will start to recognize you anywhere you go. You can create a distraction with a group of people surrounding you and asking for your autograph, shack your hand, etc. Any person who is looking for you will have Disadvantage on perception checks.

#### Rising Star
- ==15 MPP (Action)== **Light Show**: Light can always help on any show to make the audience know who shines brightest. You can create a 10ft radius circle where a light show happens and blind anyone in this area. You can also cast this with no MPP which will add a 1d4 MPP to any show you make

#### Star
- ==20 MPP (Action)== **The sound of the crowd**: During a concert the music isn't the only noise that one can hear, but the excitement and shouts of the crowd. You can create a 15 ft. cone from you. If the creature fails a constitution saving throw it'll receive 1d12 force damage and become de deafened. On a save they receive half damage

### How to lose MPP
---
Losing MPP will happen only when you stop doing stuff that a musician in search of fame would do or when you do stuff that the public doesn't like
- _-2 MPP per day when activated_ | Not making a show for some for a long time. Your level of fame will determine how much time will it take for this to take effect. The days are only days in a city
	- Starter: 2 days
	- Indie: 3 days
	- Rising Star: 4 days
	- Star: 5 days
- _-8 MPP_ | Negative impact to a fan
-  _-15 MPP_ | Putting in danger fans or cities
