## Appearance
Few have actually seen this creature real for as it changes its appearance to a loved one, but its form is that of a viscus liquid that moves with high speed and agility. Not a defined form, just a moldable creature.

## Core Description
The [Micixhua](Micixhua.md) that is located on the continent of [Ojyolco](Ojyolco.md), and lurks on the mangrove trees sliding though its branches and roots hunting abandoned creatures that have had a powerful feeling of sadness or sorrow.
# Perception
There are mixed perception on this creature. Some have reported that this creature will show you a loved one from your past, other see people that have made them feel guilt. There are those who say that this creature is grotesque and doesn't let the dead rest, others say it's the only way to reunite with loved once that left. Perceptions varies from persons and from cities.

## Diet
- Pure magic
- Magic receptors

## Predatory techniques
This Micixhua focuses on the induction of **Sadness**
* It will lurk the mangrove forest looking for sad creatures
* When identified, it starts its ritual to pray
* If an eye is present (being in the form of a painting or statue), it'll drop a black tear. This will indicate the connection between the creature and Naualikixayotl (DC 15) 
* Dark fog will surround the creature and Infront of him it's loved one or it's guilt memory.
* After doing eye contact, Naualikixayotl will receive more memories and with it, its voice
* It will influence the sadness or sorrow, until satisfied with the Pure magic build up
* Will reach out for a "hug" or catching the pray and absorbs all pure magic from it leaving behind a cocoon of what once was a living being and merges it onto the trees
