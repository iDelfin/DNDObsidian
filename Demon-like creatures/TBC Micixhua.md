## Description
This are the separated parts from [TBC The Masiosare](TBC%20The%20Masiosare.md) that are scattered around the world of Direm. They come in different forms and have their own myth and legends but all as dangerous as a loose demon on mortals.

## Diet

- Magic receptors

## Predatory techniques
It depends on the creature but all have the same intent
- Insite the prey with a powerful feeling
- When the creature is satisfied with the level of emotion, they consume their magical connection
