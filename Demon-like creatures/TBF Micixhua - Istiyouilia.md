## Basic Characteristics

- **Parent:** [TBC The Masiosare](TBC%20The%20Masiosare.md)
- **Domains:** Anti-magic, [TBC Micixhua](TBC%20Micixhua.md)
- **Symbol:** Rusted Bells over a lantern
- **Appearance:** A 3.5m slender melting dark snow man with stick like arms, an unsettling smile with coal like teeth, dark hollow eye sockets, bells hanging from one of it's arms and a very dim lantern on the other. It has the appearance of a statue as it only moves when it's not being seen.

## Core Description
The [TBC Micixhua](TBC%20Micixhua.md) that is located on the continent of [Torania](Torania.md), mainly on the south side as it lurks when snow storms arrive and hides amongst the wind. His main objective is to induce people with pure fear and absorb all pure magic that accumulates from the fear.

# Perception
It is seen as a bogyman, some say its a myth, some sure to have seen it, but only few have actually survived an encounter with it. The ones who did with the passage of time tended to think it was maybe just a dream, but, just in case, all children learn [The Snow with Eyes](The%20Snow%20with%20Eyes.md) nursery 

## Diet
- Pure magic
- Magic receptors

## Predatory techniques
This Micixhua focuses on the induction of **Fear**
- It appears during a snow storm or a dark night
- When a pray is alone with the only sound being of the wind and its cries, Istiyouilia starts it's ritual
- Faint bells start to be heard far from the pray and coming from different directions, but constantly
- A dim light appears in the distance
- When the pray starts to fasten it's breath, Istiyouilia presents itself appearing from behind the pray 5m apart. It's presence inciting fear and horror
- If the pray runs, Istiyouilia appears behind trees to let the pray no there is no escape and when Istiyouilia is satisficed with the Pure magic that is building up, it extends an arm in front of the pray, making it fall
- When the pray is physically vulnerable it absorbs all pure magic from it leaving behind a cocoon of what once was a living being and covers it in snow, like a snowman, with a smile like Istiyouilia
