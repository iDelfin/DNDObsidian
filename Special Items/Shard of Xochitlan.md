**AKA: [Shard of Xochitlan, Key of Origin]**
(Requires a wizard, sorcerer, warlock or druid to attune to it)

**Important notes**
Xochitlan: Jardín (Nahuatl [Dictionario](https://aulex.org/nahuatl/?busca=Jard%C3%ADn))

One of many shard from the different realms of Direm. This one has the curios initial property of being inactive and must be activated in a very specific ways. This shard is a "rock" that belonged to the realm of [Xochitlan](Xochitlan.md), infused with the essence of it. Depending on its state, it'll have different effects to the bearer and the world.

---
## Activate the Shard
One must find specific places in the world where magic overwhelms the place. This places varies from the [The Birthplace of Lycanthropes], [Bruniauga](Bruniauga.md), [The Tree of Life](The%20Tree%20of%20Life.md), or [The Floating Islands of Air]. Legends and myths from these places say that a powerful object or entity resides here and imbued the land with pure and powerful magic.

To activate the Shard one must
- Venture to the center of one of these places
- Place the shard in the hands of the corps of the fallen deity
- Place an object that holds a strong sentiment (Any) to this hands
Effects
- The shard and the object start shacking and floating
- The object with the strong sentiment will looks like it transform into glowing yellow pollen (amount of pollen proportional to the size)
- This pollen will swirl around the shard until it reaches the top and concentrates in one place
- After which the pollen shoots upwards with lightning speed, stops, and shoots towards the direction of the current active Shard
- After some time the pollen returns to the far top of the shard but now seams to have some but not all, blue glowing pollen
- The pollen starts falling into the shard little by little and gets absorbed into the shard in a vortex manner.
- The shard will glow with an intense light followed by it being dimed and in the shard a mirror, a mirror to [The Garden]

#### **Inactive Shard**
Physically this shard will look like another big gemstone but emitting a potent magical essence from an unknown school but definitely with magical properties. When detecting magic one can also detect that the shard is inactive
- Can attach this shard to any object in their position while attuned to it
- While attuned to it one will receive +1 to the user spellcasting attack roll and DC spellcasting saving throw
- If user stops being attuned to it, it'll fall from the object attached to it and will stop having its benefits
#### **Active Shard**
Physically the shard will still look like a big gemstone which emits an overwhelming amount of magical essence, but now it'll look like one is holding a piece of a window to another world. When looking into it one will be able to look at a blue glowing branch that spans all the gemstone and on it a lot of blue leaves and beautiful flowers in the branch but one specific flower will glow more than the rest (The Flower of `Kuauteponaualotl` from the person attuned to the shard. See [Magic system (Tree of magic and receivers)](Magic%20system%20(Tree%20of%20magic%20and%20receivers).md) for more details). When detecting magic one can detect that the shard is active.
- Can attach this shard to anu object in their position while attuned to it
- While attuned to it one will receive +2 to it's spellcasting attack and DC spellcasting saving throw
- An additional spell slot to it's highest level spell (must have a spell learned from that level, Scroll do not count)
- The Magic Circle spell once per short rest which won't use any spell slots
