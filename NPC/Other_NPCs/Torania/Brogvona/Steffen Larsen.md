This Caracter is the potion shop keeper of the city of [Brogvona](Brogvona.md). He is a very skillful alchemist that loves discovering and learning about new recipes. His main motivation is to make a potion using the [[flower of a Skrifralla]], but can't gets his hands on it because of how it should be harvested.
