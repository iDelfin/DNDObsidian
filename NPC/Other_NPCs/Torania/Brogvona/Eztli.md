He will not talk until he actually feels safe from everything and be in a state of constant fear. This creature can easily become an ally for anyone who helps him
## Backstory
### Life in Ojyolco
Eztli is from the far lands of [Ojyolco](Ojyolco.md) where he lived in the southern part of [The Navalotl Forest](The%20Navalotl%20Forest.md) with his brother Aztli, as a free Ajolotlacs discovering new things in the forest and helping the elves living there by selling exotic plant that grow in the rivers as they have a lot of expertise when it comes to swimming. The used to have long walks in the forest until finding something new, harvesting fruit and hunting for them to eat well that day. It was a good and peaceful life, but everything must come to an end, even good things. It was a cloudy day when everything was lost and Eztli's life changed since then. A group of explores from [Inglomass](Inglomass.md) appeared out of nowhere from the bushes, screaming and laughing near the house of the two Ajolotlacs. Eztli and Aztli where scared of creatures that they've never seen. After some minutes they could here how the laughter turned into whispers and the a knocking on their door. The sound of that horrible and loud knocking of wood that still haunts Eztli to this day. Aztli was in a position of lunging himself against to the intruders, but Eztli stopped him and told him to wait for him to try a more diplomatic way. He approached the door and slowly opened it and asked the creatures if he could help them with anything. In front of him was a tall and imposing half-orc with full plate of armor and a long sword that almost doubled in size Eztli. The moment the two made eye contact the half-orc's eyes widened and gave out the biggest unsettling smile as if he just found gold and will do anything to get it. In that moment Eztli felt paralyzing fear like nothing he has felt before, as if he was looking at the Deity of the Great Beyond right in his hallow eyes. Eztli could only walk backwards, away from the half-orc, it was the only thing that he could process on doing. The half-orc grabbed the door and opened it all the way and made a thunderous sound when it hit the wall. Aztli screamed to Eztli and told him to run away and lunged himself into the face of the half-orc taking one eye out of him and scratching the other but missing the mark to take it out. as Aztli preparing to lunge one more time to get the other eye he looked at Aztli and shouted again to tell Eztli to run, but that look cost him everything... Eztli in that moment could only foces on Eztli and thats when after seeing the shout, he heard silence in Aztli, saw a motionless brother in front of him and something shiny... the long sword going though him, followed by Aztli being lifted with it and thorned towards Eztli. Eztli now has the corpse of his brother right in front of him... motionless, expressionless, broken. Eztli couldn't do anything, he became frozen in the moment, could hear things but not listen, see the creature but not hear them. In that moment Eztli was only existing and experiencing fear at is full power. The invaders knocked him out and the only thing he could see while unconsent was the scene of the corpes of his brother with the lower half of the half-orc on the background.
