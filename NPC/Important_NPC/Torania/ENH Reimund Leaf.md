### Personal history

He was born here in [---Brogvona _City of Hope_](---Brogvona%20_City%20of%20Hope_.md)) from a fisherman family, one of the best fisherman in this city. while growing up, he helped his father with fishing but never truly enjoyed it. He did seems to tolerate it but he always went looking for something he liked. He grew up with his older brother Balod and younger sister Azyla. When Reimund was 10, her sister went out to the forest to play and explore to see what she could finds but she  never came back. Her family was really worried, especially Reimund. Every single day her family would go out to find her but after 12 moths the family started to accept the fact that she was gone forever, but Raimund didn't give up so every day he would go and sit in the forest in case his sister would come back. on one of those trips to the forest he saw a big dire wolf that was injured really badly. Reimund slowly walked towards the dire wolf giving out an energy of wanted to help him. This dire wolf at first started to be defensive against him and tried to scared him, but this had no effect against him. Reimund didn't give up on helping him and so after he got next to him he treated the wolf and cared about him. He visited the wolf until he could work again, bringing him food, and treating his injury. After some time the wolf stared to get up, and with the help of Reimund he could stand up and walk again. After this he did not see the wolf again, but did hear a voice nearby saying "I give you my thanks, and to that I'm in your debt". Since this moment on Reimund decided to be a Druid and that he'll help anyone he could. During his time growing up, he started to help more and more people in the city and eventually he got the support of the people to become the mayor. This moment was a very important part in his life because in his perspective this is the best way to not only help as much people as he can, but to also make sure that the city keeps in balance with nature as he did with the wolf of his past.

### Education

His education was family education and experience from life. The is no school in Borgvona but his father taught him about basic mathematics, reading, writing and a little bit of history but most of his education came from the city library.

### Employment

He is the mayor of the city of Borgvona and is responsible for trading routs, military of the city, city projects management, income of the city and the use of the money.
