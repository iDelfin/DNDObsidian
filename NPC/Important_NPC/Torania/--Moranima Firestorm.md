### Moranima Firestorm (a.k.a. Carla Longstrike) (they/them) (teafling) (NPC)

## Location
they are currently living in [Torania](Torania.md) on [---The Castle of Old](---The%20Castle%20of%20Old.md) and continuing their research in the old shed located on the garden. They are trapped behind the wall of shadow powered by fear and hatred created by the [Shadow Demon Tarlock](Shadow%20Demon%20Tarlock.md).

## Research
Their research was focused on the investigation of the new effects that feelings have over the material plane and how they coexist with living beings. They believed that feelings have a powerful effect in the world and that people could benefit from coexisting with them, like how bards take advantage of the feelings that music give. While researching she also discovered that it could be use for worst ways. They saw that feelings can be harvest for power or creating consent beings with one or more feeling in mind. Their current objective is the regeneration of feelings pulled out of others.

## Call for help
While researching about the feelings that are floating around the world, they may have discovered where feelings go when one passes away, but they also detected that some were not going in a complete form, like it was just part of the whole package that are feelings. They want to go and check on the feelings, but to do that they must collect parts for the creation of [--The Staff of Torania](--The%20Staff%20of%20Torania.md). A key that will help them open [-The Door of fjölkyngi (magic Viking)](-The%20Door%20of%20fjölkyngi%20(magic%20Viking).md) and get to the the place where unused feelings are stored. The problem is that their are many creatures in search of this door and this place for the simple raw power that feelings can have. [--Moranima Firestorm](--Moranima%20Firestorm.md) believes that one can find part of the staff on the center of Lake Aglotol, inside the cave that everyone fears. From there, they believe that the rest of the parts will be revealed.
