## Backstory
He was born and raised in [The Kingdom of Arangtor](The%20Kingdom%20of%20Arangtor.md) located in the continent of [Inglomass](Inglomass.md). While in Arangtor he was part of a very powerful family of merchants who dwindled in the selling's of costume brews for any needs, the [Dubois family](Dubois%20family.md) with the company called   . He was raised with the ideology that money it's the only thing that matter, more than friends or even family. He saw members of his family being abused for not meeting expectations, members putting money over others. After some time, he grew up and so his ability of brewing and became a big asset of his family and also his loyalty to it. He made a proposition, one that all family could benefit, but mostly him. He asked to expand the business to other continents. This with the purpose of gaining more sails from other places and with that more money. On the eye of Odger he benefits being the leader of this expansion as he will receive most of the utilities without anyone being between him and the money, not even his father. So after some long talks of business with his father, the head of the family, he convinced him to be send to [Torania](Torania.md) and expand the the family mark.
## Current work
Odger is currently working in a secret layer on the underground of [Brogvona](Brogvona.md) where he prepares fake medicine for the population of the city with the purpose of selling goods, but also works with [Riston Aleate](Riston%20Aleate.md) to add experiments to the beverages and distribute then in exchange of a lot of money.
