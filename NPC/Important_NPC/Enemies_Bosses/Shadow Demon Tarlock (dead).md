## Location
This Shadow Demon is located inside [The Castle of Old](The%20Castle%20of%20Old.md) sealed inside 3 parts of a crystal ball which are locked behind magic barriers that can only be open by the one who sealed the demon, [Moranima Firestorm](Moranima%20Firestorm.md). Now a very small influence of the demon can be felt throughout the castle as the sealing spell wasn't couldn't be cast properly and has been concentrating this influence to be a great wall of darkness, some shadow tentacles on top of the broken crystals leading to 3 rooms where this demon can extract small, replenishable, amounts of emotions from people and free his prison.

## Seal of Moranima
This Demon was created and instructed by `He who watches, He who waits` ([TBU The Preserver](TBU%20The%20Preserver.md)) to go to the [The Castle of Old](The%20Castle%20of%20Old.md) as he felt that there was an increase of feeling magic that he did not know of. The demon was instructed to investigate and retreat anything he finds. When he manifested inside the house at first he found an abandoned place with a lot of death on the ground. He search the castle first floor for a long time looking to complete his mission for [TBU The Preserver](TBU%20The%20Preserver.md). When he got to the common room of the castle he started to feel the magic to be close but was interrupted by something, or someone. A mage appeared behind the demon and fought him with the intent to kill it and leave them be, but a demon can't be easily killed and almost defeated this mage called [Moranima Firestorm](Moranima%20Firestorm.md). As a last resort of the mage almost dying, they started to cast a sealing ritual giving every single drop of energy and magic in their body. The demon tried to stop them and was able to land some attacks at them but wasn't able to stop the ritual and was sealed inside a crystal ball. [Moranima Firestorm](Moranima%20Firestorm.md) grab this ball to take it to a safe place but their wounds were a lot and dropped it which the demon did everything he had to escape it but couldn't completely. That which escaped tried to kill [Moranima Firestorm](Moranima%20Firestorm.md) but could only push them so they did and pushed to the garden of the castle and sealed them from entering by creating a shadow wall of fear and hatred. Now the demon uses his small influence in the castle to lead any adventurer to free him by taking some of their feelings.

## Partial Knowledge of [TBU The Preserver](TBU%20The%20Preserver.md)
This demon was created by [TBU The Preserver](TBU%20The%20Preserver.md) and so it has some knowledge that it has, mainly from the basic backstories of others and information of things of interests. He uses this information to struck fear into the hearts of his enemies and take advantage of it.
