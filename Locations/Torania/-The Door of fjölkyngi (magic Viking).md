## Summery
This door is the only door in [Torania](Torania.md) that leads to `The Feywild` and the residence of not only all the magic in Torania, but of [---Jörmungrandr](---Jörmungrandr.md). This door was made by[---Jörmungrandr](---Jörmungrandr.md) himself in case of an emergency where he would need to go to the mortal realm, or if something or one needed to communicate with him but only should be used for that purpose, because if the wrong person would enter this door, it could easily disrupted all the magic on [Torania](Torania.md) if it wanted to and contain it in something, granting him with massive power that would match the deities of Direm. For that reason [---Jörmungrandr](---Jörmungrandr.md) made the Archway very difficult to discover and access. Unfortunately when he build the door a beautiful forest surrounded the door because pure natural magic started to flow though and create life, making it a big landmark and creating [TBR The Ice Forest (Depricated)](TBR%20The%20Ice%20Forest%20(Depricated).md). [---Jörmungrandr](---Jörmungrandr.md) couldn't destroy the forest because it is against her mothers ideal and he knew it would grow back again so he needed to find a way to keep creatures away from here, and here's where the Dark Mist comes in. A mist created by [---Jörmungrandr](---Jörmungrandr.md) with the ability to envelope any living being who enters the forest and taking them back to the way they enter. If one does pass the Dark Mist be it from pure will or with the [--The Staff of Torania](--The%20Staff%20of%20Torania.md), they will find the door which is a stone archway in the middle of a small hill which is surrounded by water and in the air, remains of the Dark mist floats around, making the air really heavy.
There is only one way that this door may be open, and that's is using [--The Staff of Torania](--The%20Staff%20of%20Torania.md). By bringing the staff to the archway, the individual materials will break own, fusing themselves to the top of the archway and melting into an ooze like substance, creating the womb that leads to `The Feywild`.
