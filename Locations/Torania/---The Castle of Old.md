## Puzzles
---
Inside the castle one will find three different puzzles witch are necessary to complete for one to be shown the door to the garden. The way the group will find these puzzles will by by means of black smoke coming out of 3 pedestals in the main room of the castle. These puzzles will be located in different rooms in the 2nd floor of the castle and the doors will have the black smoke going though the middle.

---
### The music puzzle
This room will have the feeling of a study room. It has a lot of books in bookshelf  and on the far side of the room one will find a resting place with some couches, a fireplace and a piano. In the fireplace one can find some things of interest as one will see above it a frame with a music sheet containing notes and surrounding it 3 unlit torches (on the lift, right and top). Other stuff that the group may find in the room will be instruments laying on the floor near the fire place like a lute and a broken violin.

#### Solution
The solution of this puzzles is to play the song that is in the frame and it must be 3 instruments at the same time.
The song is:
`Do^ Fa La Re Sib Mi Fa`

#### Tip
If the players are taking to long to figure the solution out, a small rock will fall from the celling and hit one of the strings in the lute and when that happens, the right torch will light up for a brief second.

This song, if played by three as well at any moment or in the same round, any allies will gain a 1d4 psychic damage for it's next attack

#### Notes
* Every time a note is played, a light of the torches will lit up
* If a new instrument is played, it'll ignite a torch that has not been lit and it'll have a small explosion of ignition
	* If all torches have been lit, it'll replace the oldest instrument.

---
### The potion puzzle
This room will have the feeling of a laboratory with various unfinished experiments and marks of the past. It'll have broken test tubes, dry ingredients, explosion marks, dry liquid substances and what appears to be observation rooms. On the room on the left one will find some potion bottles laying on the table with some burned marks and some broken glass. On the room on the right one will find some carvings on the table, difficult to deduce what it is as it has been slashed by some claws. Another thing that one will notice is two bodies in this room. One having indication of being killed by claws, and the other having indication that it was burned from the inside. The one that was burned was a Lycanthrope.
The moment that the party enters the room, the fire below the caldron in the center of the room will lit up and the candle in the left room will also lit up but in a Magenta color. One think that also will happen is that an obvious glow will appear on top of the 3 colored ingredients.

#### Solution
The solution of this puzzle is to combine the ingredients to get the correct color indicated by the flame on the candle. First the left room will have the candle lit in magenta and so one must combine the red and blue ingredients to create a **magenta**. After making the combination the flame will have a big pulse followed by the fire being unlit and then the candle on the right room will be lit on a **yellow** color and the caldron fire will follow. After this one, will follow the last combination which will lit both candles and become a white fire.
