This forest is a peculiar one, as it is one that is located in the artic biome of [Torania](Torania.md), a place where no vegetation should but yet trees can be seen from afar. This place gives most people a sensation of uneasiness as they are seeing something that shouldn't be there, life just standing, almost as if it was staring right back at you.

## Closer look
---
As one get's closer to this forest they may notice something that seams weird from the place. The trees are completely frozen in place. The forest is not a place full of flora and fauna, it's a place from the past frozen in here. The trees are preserved and but motionless, behind the thick layer of ice, one will find a once lifeful tree and flora in a place where it would not thrive.

## Importance
---
This forest has great importance in [Torania](Torania.md) as it is the fortress to defend [-The Door of fjölkyngi (magic Viking)](-The%20Door%20of%20fjölkyngi%20(magic%20Viking).md) from any unwanted people to enter. The parts on the surface may be frozen, but the roots are still active, attacking any uninvited intruders trying to get to the door and maintaining it closed. This forest is also responsible for healing the protectors of [Torania](Torania.md), the 2 Dragons that protect the continent from great evils if it must come to that. If this forest, for some reason, disappear, or is killed, it would have catastrophic consequences. To begin the with, it would leave [-The Door of fjölkyngi (magic Viking)](-The%20Door%20of%20fjölkyngi%20(magic%20Viking).md) un protected from anyone who would try and get the pure power of feelings stored in the `Underdark` and leave at least all of [Torania](Torania.md)'s new born without feelings, as there the world have non to give, for at least 3 years. It would also mean that the protectors of [Torania](Torania.md) won't have a place to heal when they face a big enemy to the verge of death and possibly succumb to their wounds and leave the continent unprotected from big forces of evil. 

## Properties
---
The roots work as ground sensor and when a creature sets foot on the forest, a small wave of natural magic can be seen concentrated on the foot of the creature. This is the forest trying to read the intention of the creature, what is it's purpose in the forest. One may do a wisdom saving throw to resist the reading of intention, but at a cost on untrustiest from part of the forest and may turn against you. Once inside the forest they will be presented with a place full of trees and will start the judgement of the forest. If the forest sees that you do not have a pure intention in the forest, it'll surround one with a thick fog and slowly move the ground around you so that one may end up exciting the forest from where they came from. If one insist on entering the forest or damages something from it, it'll surround the creature in a fog once again but this time, it'll take it to a special place. a place to destroy the uninvited guest.
