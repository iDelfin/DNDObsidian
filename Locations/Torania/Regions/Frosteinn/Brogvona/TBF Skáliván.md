# **Skáliván (skáli- Hall, -ván Hope) (Hall of Hope) (City Hall)**

## Map

## Details
- The central building that is responsible for managing the whole city
- Advisors offices can be found on the 2nd floor with a small library containing historical events and executive logs
- On the fir floor one can find a collection of paintings that describes the colonization of Torania
- [ENH Reimund Leaf](ENH%20Reimund%20Leaf.md)'s office can be found here on the 3rd floor
- Reimund's most trusted advisors are here
- Has a mess hall for official business with other diplomats or governmental bodies
- It seams to have a very family vibe as all who work here seams to have known each other from way back
