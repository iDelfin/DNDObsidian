This boat is a beautiful "3 story" boat which is located on the lake Aglotol near the docks of [Brogvona](Brogvona.md) and imposes it's presence to any who doubts it. One can easily sea that this boat is made of 3 sail with 8 cannons on each side which seams to be inactive or at least not being a priority. 
## Outside

On this floor one will find various things that seams to be laying around, but mainly boxes, lots and lots of boxes. Some containing `Fake medicin` bottles and other with illicit substances that ranges from hallucinogenic mushrooms, to drug from various plants. Another thing that one will find are 6 cannons pointing outside of the boat next to a box full of cannon balls. On the center there is the main sail which has a crow's nest on it which responsibility has been passed down to [---Roseberry](---Roseberry.md). There is also a capstan which is in a deployed position. Meaning that the anchor is loose and maintaining the boat still.
