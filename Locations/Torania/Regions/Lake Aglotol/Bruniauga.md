## Summery
This chasm is the entrance to one of 4 [---Emotion Dispersal Systems (EDS)] which is responsible for delivering emotions to creatures born on or near the surrounding area of [Torania]. This giant mushroom ecosystem consist of a "Mother Tree" and various mushrooms that varies in species and effects. What makes these mushrooms special are that their spores are not use mostly for reproduction, but as the [---Emotion Dispersal Systems (EDS)]. The mushroom shoot out balls of emotions out and they float towards the entrance which they become so rounded by a small midbrain layer of heat to protect them from the cold climate and travel throughout [Torania](Torania.md).

## Entrance
---
### Description
From the outside one will start to feel a small natural heat coming from the chasm and a bright burning red light. Most of the people who get near here they get scared because of the superstition of believing that it's the entrance to "Hell", "The Fire Plane", etc. If one does collect their fear and enters the grotto they will find like a path going around the walls of this chasm and they will be treated with the reveal that the source of the heat is actually from mushrooms that appear to be sticking out from the walls and emits a bright red color from inside which is "Divine Magic", no "Natural Magic", wait, it's "Feeling Magic". This red light is the responsible of the membrane that surrounds the `Spores of Emotions` coming of the [---Emotion Dispersal Systems (EDS)]. At the end of the entrance, before entering the EDS itself, one must be careful as they may find that the mushrooms on the floor which shines a different color of red. This is an explosive mushroom. This mushroom acts like a used feeling detector that will grow the closer a creature gets to it until it explodes and deals 3d6 Psychic damage as a surge of emotions hits the creature (if the creature is a Lycantrope/Vampire, it must make it's wisdom saving throw of it's "Hunger of Blood" thanks to the surge of emotions. Any other creature will be affected by the random feeling table `Figure 1.1`)

### Ruins
The work of [Manook'in] to symbolize one of EDS they protected and investigated. This ruins doesn't consist of much as time and nature have taken it's toll. One will find different stone tables filled with vines and moss on the north and north west side wall. Another ruin that one will find is some small stones on the ground that blend with the moss of the ground. When having a closed look to it one can conclude that it may have served a type of dome structure. Its purpose is very difficult to figure out as it seams like some pieces have been completely ruined by time. One important thing does remain somewhat legible. A stone slab with what seams to be old text that was carved to it. The only legible part of it is the part that was deeply carved into the stone, which isn't much. If translated, it reads `...04...Fire_eye...status...` and on the corner of this stone slab the emblem of [Manook'in].
