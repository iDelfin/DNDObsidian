## Room 1 | Entrence
This is the main entrance of the layer for the deb for the development of fake medicine. This part has a feeling of being a cave as the walls are not smooth and there are still wooden pillars on the sealing. The only thing that seams smooth is the wall to the entrance
### Mushrooms
The mushrooms in here are all poisonous

## Room 2 | Hallway
This is a long hallway with an aery feeling of loneliness... but still feeling that one is being watch. One will find various containers just being there as to indicate a lack of space elsewhere. If one opens them, they will find bulk of materials that ranges from herbs, to water and... other herbs. This hallway connects all other rooms to each other

---
## Room 3 | Communication Room
The door of this room is closed and locked and must required lockpicking to be open or kill the innocent [---Eztli](---Eztli.md) that is captured against his will just to clean the rooms. In this room one will find a lot of messaging stones (a total of 8) which 2 can be seen to be active. One will also find 2 chest that seem to be vert well preserved but locked. If one does open it one will find that it contains the coins, and lots of it `100 GP & 50 SP` in one of them. In another one will find various filled potion bottles that ranges from 2 Healing Potions to 2 sleep potions and one bottled filled with a very specific herb, a herb used as a drug. Another thing that someone could find in this room is a bunch of letters, letters that ranges from orders for fake medicine, orders for drugs and complaints. But one letter will stand out, one out of all, as it is places on a obvious places and is highly decorated. One that will read "Instructions for plan". If open one will read the following `I need you to test a new concution for the "medicin", I'll send you a new liquid as soon as posible, it'll contain instructions on how to combine it with the already used medicin. Add this to the solution and report the effects. You know what happens when you don't. ~R.A.`

---
## Room 4 | Cleaning Room
This room is a basic cleaning room where one will find from cloths and a bucket filled with water and dirt. There is also a dirty mod and a "escoba", but something else can be seen here. When a player opens the door to this room, the'll need to roll a perception check against a +4 stealth check. If succeeded they will see [---Eztli](---Eztli.md) trying to hide from them with a look of terror and a pose of protections as if he was prepared to be hit by something.

---
## Room 5 | Armory
This room is one with a curious form as it is not like the other rooms but they will find that is full of weapons, armors and shields ready to be used, and some still being repaired. along the entrance side wall one will find basic weapons that rages from longsword (2), short swords(4), battleaxes (2), and hand axes(3). On the side walls there will be 2 chests which one is locked (left) and the other is open. The open will be filed with various shield that ranges from wooden (2) and metal (4). In the other treasure chest one will find a very curious artefact, a very special one, and because of that, one must succeed on a DC 15. Inside this chest there will be a quarterstaff, but one that emits magic, as if it's embedded with something and used for offence `Quarterstaff (Hex per short rest)`. The various armors that one will find displayed are "Ring mail", "Padded Armor" & 2 "Chain shirts".

--- 
## Room 6 | Main Chamber
This is the main room of all the HQ as it is where not only where the organization creates the fake medicine, but also where the leader counts it's coins and supervised the creation of the concoction. There will be 7 total enemies in here
- 1 Bandit captain
- 6 Bandits
When killed and searched this will be found
### Bandits
They will find their weapons and gear for combat, but on 2 or 3 of them, one can find 2 GP and some loose herbs.
