A place which has been dominated by giant mushrooms which originally wasn't this big. This happen at the same time as when [TBF The Tree of Life](TBF%20The%20Tree%20of%20Life.md) came to be (near the beginning of Direm) as this was another way the grand accumulation of magic manifested itself.

This forest is home of to many magical creatures thanks to it's magical *feeling* in it. Creatures feel as one to this forest, some have even say that is some sort of nostalgia which attracts them to live in here. The main reason of this is because of the origin of these creatures as most fauna in this forest are direct decedents of the original inhabitants of the `faywild` and the concentration of this magic that created this place is from the `faywild`.
