## Beginning
---
### Location
This side quest is given by a musician be it from Brogvona or Brogkunnusta and will be developed in that city of [Torania](Torania.md).

### Quest giving
One can get to this quest by talking to the musician directly and she will ask for help. When the NPC talks to the party, they'll talk to the party about how they hates this group of people and how the group are treating badly to other residences, just because of their power over the `medicin`. Then they'll ask the party something really simple, they'll ask for the party to steel there harp from the group. they'll mention where they last seen it and will talk about the reward, which will go in a way of offering stuff without monetary value because they doesn't have anything

## Development
---
### Summery
The NPC will tell the party that they saw the harp in one of the rooms in the tavern. They will also tell the party that they saw the person who stole their harp leave the tavern but who knows for how long. They would do it but they don't have the skills to sneak to get their harp. The party must sneak though the tavern and steal the harp that will be on the chest of the room. After some time the owner of the room will come back and will try to get in, if the owner of he room does find anyone in the party they will be reported to the authorities, will have a bounty on them of 30GP each and the harp will be sent somewhere else and open up another side quest

### Specifics of the room
The room will have what it normally has but one will also find various item laying around, from lose coins, to coats and, most importantly, a letter. The letter will have some writing that will read the following:
```
Roots

We have much to speek about the future of the bussines. Please meet us on the lillypad and make sure that no soul sees this message.

Seed to seed...
Dust to dust

R~
```

## End
---
This side quest will be considered finished when the party delivers the harp to the musician NPC. They will thank the party so much and give the reward to them and, depending on the interaction of the NPC, will be forever in the parties debt. That debt will be remembered by the NPC.
