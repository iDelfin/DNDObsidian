## Intro description

The world of Direm is filled with many mysteries and one of those is the origin of all magic. Magic fills with wonders and found in places one would never expect. Embedded in the roots of all trees to snowy hills formed in the deepest part of the snow dessert. One place filled with these mysteries is the continent of [Torania](Torania.md), a continent with a really young society who is little by little growing and discovering what this continent has to offer. During this era of discovery something peculiar happens in the capital city of [Brogvona](Brogvona.md), a beam of light flights though the sky like a shooting star and lands on the top most part of the [Skáliván](Skáliván.md). The mayor of the city has asked adventurers in search of riches and adventure to venture to [The Castle of Old](The%20Castle%20of%20Old.md). What you'll find on this adventure may reveal secrets of the past or truths of the present.

*Requirements*
- *Follower(s) or believer(s) of the Goddess of Nature or children of it* (Optional)

- During the fight with the shadow demon, one may do a free perception check
- [Perception 15] One will notice that there is a battle occurring on the other side of the doors blockade by the shadow wall

- [Constitution 10] Any creature at least 10ft next to any of the shockwave origin must make a Constitution saving throw
- When exiting the castle one will find different things depending on the time of exit

### If the party exit immediately
- The party will see the beautiful but small garden belonging to the owner of the castle
- An unknown clocked figure running behind the right building and over its shoulder, the motionless body of an adult woman. 
- [Perception 10] The unknown figure will have a small moment where it's eye will be seen with the reflection of the snow. *{The eyes of the father that abandoned the player}* will shine into you and hastily turn away. 
- Additional to that, the player will notice the motionless body is unconscious and not dead.
- Then, a small flash of light will appear behind the right building (where the unknown figure ended)

### If the party waits a little to exit (at least go to another room before exiting)
- The party will see the beautiful but small garden belonging to the owner of the castle
- One will find scorch marks on the grown and on the walls of the buildings. There was a battle in this parts of the castle between two powerful warriors. 
- [Perception/Investigation 10] This battle was between a wizard with specific magic (Moranima if player with connection perceives or is told about it) and a warlock.
- There will be foot marks on the ground on the far right side of the garden and will end behind the right building until one sees a small patch of missing tall grass.
- Another thing that will be notice is that Moranima is nowhere to be seen.

### No matter when the party exits

![MoranimaLabHutCOF](MoranimaLabHutCOF.png)

	- The place where Moranima rests and personal items lies
	- A **bedside table** with a bowl containing bread crumbs, a set of keys that open the Lab and the greenhouse un front

	- A globe of the world where one can see the 4 continents of [Torania, Inglomass, Oyolco and Farlon]

	- Inside the boxes one will find on the left side more apples, some berries. On the right some meting ice with a faint smell of fish.
	- Next to the basket, a contraption made of glass pointing to a bucket half full of water
		- [Intelligence 15] This contraption has the purpose to filter water though means of vapor. The water evaporates, floats to the other flask and condensates to the bucket.
	- On the south side a table with a small candle with two chairs, one at an angle and another tucked into the table.

	- On the northside, a pair of tables. On the left side, an empty dirty caldron, and a bucket that seams recently filled. On the right side some sort of runes have been carved onto the table, some tools that seams to range from pinches and scoops, and some research papers (See Moranima's research papers p.1,p.5)

		- [Arcana 20] Additional to the 10 info one will notice the purpose of the rune. It is to revile the flow of magic into living beings.

		- [Investigation 10] 
			- One will find some ingredients for a healing potion (if 20, ingredients for 2 healing potions)
			- One will also find a mysterious arcane like glow under the table. A small DIY cabinet can be found containing [Shard of Xochitlan](Shard%20of%20Xochitlan.md)
		- One will also see a glowing orb floating on top of a table. Magic gathered and concentrated into one place. When touched the orb will flow into the arm of the one who contacted it and will level up. The orb will shrink ({size of party} uses)

		- [Arcana 15] The runes are those of feeling amplifiers. When touched one will have amplified a feeling depending on the runes touch. 15 is 1 identified rune, 16 is 2 runes and so on until 20 and the 6 runes.
