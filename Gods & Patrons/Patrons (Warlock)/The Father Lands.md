## How it became a Patron
[ Depricated ]
During the beginning of Direm, after the gods created the planet and the continents, [The Goddess of Nature](The%20Goddess%20of%20Nature.md) was wondering how would she manage the life of all the creatures and life in the planet without also abandoning her other tasks, and that's when she had the idea of creating the The Father Lands. A group of "Dragons" that would be in charge of the life of each continent and forming molding it. And so the 4 Dragons were created and sent to `the Feywild` to build the life that would go to habitat Direm. `The Feywild` is a mirrored version of the material plane but with more natural magic thanks to the powers and effects of them of [The Goddess of Nature](The%20Goddess%20of%20Nature.md) and more magical structures. Each dragon would reside on the continent they are responsible in specific places where they have created portals to the material plane so that their creations would cross and habit Direm. Al though they have finish habituating Direm they haven't close the portal as they roam around the material world to judge how well life is doing. They transform into different being, they could be a wolf roaming alone, a pixie just flying around and playing with other creatures or even humanoids enjoying life and nature. When The Father Lands must discus something so that they resolve conflict then they will meet in the middle of Direm, in [TBF The Great Column](TBF%20The%20Great%20Column.md) where [The Goddess of Nature](The%20Goddess%20of%20Nature.md)'s magic is most powerful and where they can, if they want to, interact with other planes.

[ New story ]
As the people left the original land from the stories of the [Origin of the first sapient](Origin%20of%20the%20first%20sapient.md) the Gods had to think of a way to save their creations. They think and worked until they finished with something unprecedented. They created the Father Lands, great and wonderful creatures with the ability to move land from Olinyotltlan/Semanauakikpatlan to the material plane, the only once who are known to move physical things between plains.
