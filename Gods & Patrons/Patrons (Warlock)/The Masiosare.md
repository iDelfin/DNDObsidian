## Basic Characteristics

- **Domains:** Anti-magic
- **Symbol:** Black Liquid Flame
- **Appearance:** Moves in a ooze form like oil and when it's not moving, liquid flames ignites and forms a oozing creature that is described as legs of a spider, body of a serpent, upside-down head of an antler, claws of a bear. All covered in black liquid fire.

## Core Description
The literal representation of anti-magic, it's attracted to any source of magic. At his full potential it's attracted to [Kuauteponaualotl](Kuauteponaualotl.md), in smaller version, it's any source of magic equal to its power.
