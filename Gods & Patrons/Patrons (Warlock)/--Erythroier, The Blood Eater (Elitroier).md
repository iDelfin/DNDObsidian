## How it became a Patron
___
During the beginning of civilization there was a lot of conflict between tribes and villages. Many unjustly behavior was occurring around every corner of Direm and [---The God_dess of Balance](---The%20God_dess%20of%20Balance.md) was getting tired of having to judge so many people at once. So one day he created a humanoid like creature, with the appearance of a fallen Aasamar and gave it many powers for it to help control the mortals in there conflicts and called it Justice Giver. The God_dess sent him down and started to eradicate anyone who would start these conflicts. At the beginning it was going well, unjustly people were taking care of and the God_dess had less to do, until. Justice Giver started to gain satisfaction from taking the life of others and liking the taste of the blood of mortals. With this satisfaction and pleasure, greed started to manifest and so it began to look for ways to harvest the blood of mortal and use it for greater power. Justice Giver started to meditate to harvest this blood into something and after 10 long years he made it, he created a form of magic with the use of blood and only blood. He started to kill not only unjustly people, but also innocent people, justifying that it was only collateral damage. Every time he would kill, he would take their blood and absorb it to gain more and more power. after some time [---The God_dess of Balance](---The%20God_dess%20of%20Balance.md) started to realize what was going on, that Justice Giver was killing without justification, just for the fun of it. So the God_dess descended to the material plane and confronted Justice Giver. It told the God_des that his name is no longer Justice Giver, that now it is Erythroier, The Blood Eater. The God_dess, discussed of what he became, and of his name, battled him in hopes of deafening him in [---The Battle of Fire](---The%20Battle%20of%20Fire.md). This battle was a heated one, in a literal sense, you see fire is a fear of Erythroier and The God_dess knew about this and utilized it as it's maximum. When the God_des cornered Erythroier he trapped him in a sphere like object and hid it in random cave in Inglomas.
[---The God_dess of Balance](---The%20God_dess%20of%20Balance.md) does not know that this patron is still active. He thinks he can't do anything and forgot about it.
