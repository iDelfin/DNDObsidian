This goddess is in charge of things related to time and making sure that nothing disturbs it. The moment that she senses a disturbance in the waters of time, she will dash to the problem and take care of it as fast as possible. She isn’t seen very often in the world and tries to avoid interaction with anything in the “mortal world” but does talk to the “Chosen ones”. She will choose only some people that she’ll grant power to and they will inform here about the “mortal world”. Some "Chosen ones" have come together and organize to from the religion of [TBF The Flowing Sand](TBF%20The%20Flowing%20Sand.md).

## Personality
Silent and serious with her work, always putting attention to the waters of time and maintaining it's constant and steady flow forward. It is very rare for her to leave her attention from the waters of time, some can argue that it doesn't happen.

## Powers
- Time: The goddess can manipulate time as simple as that. Move time forward, backwards, stop it, accelerate it, etc. Time moves at her will.
- Timeless: She can remove the effects that time has on anything that she wants. or even protect them/it from her own power of time.
