# Overview
---
---
A group of people that dedicate themselves to the scientific investigation of the inner workings of the world. They share their findings and try to make sense of everything. This group is formed by wizards, historians, artificers and anyone who has the curiosity to find out about the world. All members are powerful mages and are a force to be reckon with, but non will use their power for conflict, just defense.

# Details
---
---
**Leader:** There isn't one specific leader but [Moranima Firestorm](Moranima%20Firestorm.md) is the most knowledgeable of all the members
**Symbol:** The head of a raven on the cover of a book.
> This makes reference to [The All-Knower](The%20All-Knower.md) deity and all members should carry this symbol (by any means: a carving, neckless, tattoo, etc.) to confirm that they are sharing the knowledge to this group of people. This is because they know that some of the information may be world changing.

## Active Missions
---
- [[Book Master Library]]
	- This is the main goal of the organization. To create a library with all the knowledge of how the world works
	- Every little discovery, experiment, and report is send to this secret library and placed as books
- Creatures of the past
	- Some have started to investigate on the finding of people that where believed to be dead but now have surfaced with a grotesque form of themselves.
	- Another thing to mention is that their emotions have multiplied. Some say that they appeared angrier, distraught, or even "happier" but in an uncontrollable way.
	- Members have isolated most of the cases but are sure that they'll find more to come.

## Vows
---
They follow moral vows to each other which are the following
- The knowledge I gain will be share amongst the order
- Any knowledge shared to the world must come from the order
- All shared knowledge must be given as ingredients and not as a final product 
- The order will not use their knowledge or power for intended conflict, only for the orders defense
- The order shall protect the library at all cost
- The order shall protect the power of knowledge at all cost
- The symbol will protect the knowledge withing the mind
> All members have recited their vows at the initiation and swore to follow them every single one of them
## Symbol
---
In the [vows](#vows) it is mention that
>*"The symbol will protect the knowledge within the mind"*

This is because their symbol is actually a glyph that, when activated it, triggers a "special" effect in the body of the one wearing it.
- From the outside the user will start to express or just internally feel emotions overwhelm their mind
- It'll start to become deafening and some even break into laughter, sorrow, or anger (depends on the user)
- Their body starts to deform in a grotesque way, elongating some parts, inflating others or creating new extensions but never to much as it always gets to the final part before manifesting
- A silent pop is heard and the eyes of the user are filled with blood. The users brain bursts completely. 
This effect has been name [[Emotional Despair]]. The first deadly effect discovered by the order and the first experiment made by [Moranima Firestorm](Moranima%20Firestorm.md)

---
