This organization is a secret one that has two different areas so that they stay in secret and be effective with their objective. They are a medium to small organization that is most commonly known as an information religion. This is something weird for most people in the world as they say they serve the Goddess of Punishment, so why do they concentrate on information. Thanks to this uncertainty of what they do, a lot of theories have come up on what they actually do, being far from the truth. Some say they use the information to inform the Goddess of Punishment about the situation of the world, others say that they use the information to sell it to other religions and they use the name of religion to hide this fact. The real job that this organization does is that they judge people with secrecy and action and help the Goddess of Punishment to do her job in the world. They are the vigilantes of this world and they use the information they retrieve during the day so that they can go at night and judge the people that must be judged. They don’t want to grow as the growth of an organization means more visibility of their job, which would lead to the government knowing that most of those governments would go against them and would make things even more difficult. They are pretty tight with admissions and if one wants to enter they must have five people saying why you should enter the organization.
